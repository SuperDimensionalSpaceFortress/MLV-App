### mlv-app, 2026-11-28 — Venue stage assembles ask the machine HeavySlot for `--class exclusive-short` and are admitted ahead of young shared pytest batches

**Before:** `tools/coordination/build-guard.ps1` hard-coded `--class shared`, so a venue stage assemble (the build-guarded make inside `playback-attr-3-cuda-assemble.ps1`) queued oldest-first behind every shared ticket, mostly 30 min pytest batches. The 40 newest hub-labelled build tickets in the heavy-slot ledger waited a median of 36.5 min (max 83.4) and held the slot a median of 1.19 min (max 8.39). heavy-slot already has the class for this (README `exclusive-short`: next job boundary, ahead of shared tickets younger than 60 min, never preempts, eta 15 min or less; a unit past 15 min demotes its label's first two segments for good).
**Now:** the assembler passes `@{ HeavySlotClass = 'exclusive-short'; HeavySlotEtaMinutes = 12 }` (12 = ceil(8.39) + 3). The class is an explicit option only (Command mode: `-HeavySlotClass`), never an environment variable, so it cannot leak into a nested guard call. Only the exact strings `shared` (default) and `exclusive-short` are accepted, ordinal (`Exclusive-Short`, a leading space or a trailing soft hyphen is refused), and exclusive-short needs an eta above 0 and at most 15, never clamped. A refusal is `BUILD_HEAVY_SLOT_REFUSED reason=invalid-option ... HeavySlotClass=<raw>`, exit 2, heavy-slot never started; it comes before the shim lookup, so a host without heavy-slot refuses too, and Command mode's eta and wait are doubles, so 15.49 is not rounded to 15. The heavy label is `mlv:venue-stage:<lane>`, so the one demotion key is `mlv:venue-stage`, never `mlv:<lane>:build` or `mlv:hub:*`. It prints `BUILD_HEAVY_SLOT_CLASS label=... class=... eta=...m`. Every other caller stays `shared`; the machine HeavySlot is untouched.
**Evidence:** RED on the base commit: 25 new guard tests fail (no class line, class and label ignored, nothing refused); GREEN with the change. Read-only against the installed tool, dot-sourced under a scratch state directory, `Get-QueueOrder` puts a shared stage ticket behind two young shared tickets last, a promotable exclusive-short one first, an eta-16 one last, and keeps a shared ticket aged 61 min ahead of the promoted one. A static pin holds the assembler's eta at 15 or less.
**Narrowed promise:** the stage still waits for the current holder and for shared tickets that have waited 60 min or more. A demotion is the owner's to clear in heavy-slot's `demoted.json`; until then the stage orders as shared again, today's behaviour. Live proof is the first staged build after merge: its ledger admit line with class `exclusive-short` and its `waitedMs`. If that is not enough, the fleet route is the Phase 2 reserve.
