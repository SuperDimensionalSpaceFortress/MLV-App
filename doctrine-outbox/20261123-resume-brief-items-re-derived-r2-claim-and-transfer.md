## mlv-app/resume-brief-items-re-derived-r2-claim-and-transfer
rule: after an account rotation the unattended tick may resume before a new hub chat. The chat treats every brief or handoff item as UNKNOWN until it has read every ruling in force, even pre-rotation ones, and the pending list. Another actor owns a card through a pending or fired row or running receipt of any round, or a ruling that names another actor to arm it; the chat does not arm such a card unless a later ruling assigns this round to the chat. Items a ruling only queued (no row, no owner) are the chat's to arm if it ran that card's previous review (here or inherited). After a fired row a retry takes a new round name. Just before arming it re-reads rulings and list, then claims through mlv-app/two-hub-actors-must-claim-a-round-before-arming-it; until that step is live it arms only while no tick runs. The claim tests the exact round only, so the chat then re-reads and cancels its row, if still pending, when another actor owns the card and no later ruling assigns this round to the chat. This narrows the window: a row can fire between the read and the cancel, and a beat past its fire gate can launch a cancelled row. A transfer writes the ruling first, then the new row, superseding a pending same-round row only; under a new round name the old pending row stays until cancelled, and may still fire until the launcher reads rulings. Local evidence; siblings adopt or distinguish.
mechanism: two of five brief items had finished four days earlier; the ledger shows the tick lifted the hold, dispatched three fix rounds and armed a P1 card before the chat's first arm. Two queued FINAL rounds had no row; the chat armed them, having run their previous keys. This rotation only.
