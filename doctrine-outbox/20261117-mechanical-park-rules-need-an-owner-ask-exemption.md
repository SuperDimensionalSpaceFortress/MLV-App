## mlv-app/mechanical-park-rules-need-an-owner-ask-exemption
rule: a card filed from an owner ask carries origin=owner on its queue row, and every mechanical park or prune rule (an intake ratio guard, an age-and-kind backlog park, a priority-age prune list) skips that row. Only a named hub ruling that names the card may park it, and the queue tool refuses any other park of it with a typed exit. A park whose note names a blocker PR ("waits for #n", "stacked on #n", "after #n") records that PR on the row, and the queue tool returns the row to queued, at its old priority, when the PR merges. The roadmap counts owner asks whatever their kind and lists the ones still owed in their own parked section, with reason and age, capped at 25 rows plus a count line so the file keeps its byte budget. An owner park is closed only by a structured resolution on the row (done, duplicate, superseded, no_go or refused), which only the queue tool's explicit park command writes; the park note is never read for completion words (deny by default, as mlv-app/spelling-chase-scanner-needs-a-deny-by-default-name).
mechanism: an owner product ask sat parked 10 days. Three mechanical rules had no owner exemption, the ask was filed with kind unclassified so the product protections skipped it, nothing re-read parked rows, and its blocker PR merged one day after the park. Parked rows are invisible to a "what is queued" search, so nobody saw it until the owner asked again. A first cut classified park notes as terminal from words like DONE or shipped and drew three adversary rounds of new spellings ("after #123 has shipped", "hasn't shipped", "not DONE" split by a newline), so the words were dropped for a closed field. Price: a wider owner match keeps more rows out of the prune list; a delivered ask stays on the owed list until the hub states its resolution once; a resolved park never re-queues, and a hub-written park reason is never overridden from the source line.
applies: mlv-app
check: py -3 -m unittest tools.coordination.test_render_roadmap -v covers the roadmap side (OwnerAskTests: an owner ask of any kind is counted, a parked one is listed with reason and age, one with a resolution is not, a non-owner tooling row is still not counted; R2AdversaryTests: terminal words without a resolution stay owed, a bad resolution exits 2, 112 parked owner rows fit the budget). The queue side is board-local: Test-OwnerAskParkGuard.ps1 beside Update-CardQueue.ps1 (an owner row survives each park rule, a parked row re-queues on its blocker merge, a non-owner row is parked exactly as before).
