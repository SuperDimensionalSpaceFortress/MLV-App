### mlv-app, 2026-11-08 — Staging build lost its machine HeavySlot turn: the wait is now the driver's deadline, not a fixed 60 min

**Before:** `tools/coordination/build-guard.ps1` always passed `--wait 60m` to heavy-slot. A venue staging build (`playback-attr-3-cuda-assemble.ps1`, called by a stage chain that retries a capacity refusal every 300 s) was refused at 60 min (heavy-slot code 75), printed `BUILD_HEAVY_SLOT_REQUEUE`, and the next attempt took a NEW ticket behind every newer waiter. The slot itself admits oldest first, so the turn was lost on the caller's side. Observed 2026-11-08: one staging build timed out 11:12:39Z-12:12:39Z and re-queued at 12:16:48Z as #2 of 6; it staged only at 13:10:18Z, about 2 h after the first request.
**Now:** the caller passes its own deadline (`Invoke-BuildGuard -Options @{ HeavySlotDeadlineUtc = ... }`, the assembler's `-HeavySlotDeadlineUtc`, or env `MLV_HEAVY_SLOT_DEADLINE_UTC`; the option wins) and `--wait` is what is left of it: 0 once passed, one week at most. `Invoke-VenueStageNightly.ps1` exports its own deadline to the assembler. No deadline: the 60 min default is unchanged, and a non-staging caller is unaffected. The guard prints `BUILD_HEAVY_SLOT_WAIT_BOUND label=<l> wait=<n>m source=deadline deadline=<o> default=60m utc=<o>` before it starts heavy-slot (only when a deadline set the bound). An unparsable deadline is `BUILD_HEAVY_SLOT_REFUSED reason=invalid-option`, exit 2, nothing launched. The machine HeavySlot is not edited (machine-owned).
**Evidence:** RED on fork/master `0a553b4e`: a scripted queue (`queued` mode of the heavy-slot test stub, 130 min of work ahead, no real time passing) given a deadline 240 min out still got `--wait 60m` and `BUILD_HEAVY_SLOT_REQUEUE`; the retry was a different ticket. GREEN: the same call gets `--wait` of about 240m and is admitted on its first ticket. Strict-mode hosts read the option safely (a missing key threw under `Set-StrictMode -Version Latest` until the default key was added). Live proof is the first staged build after merge: read its `BUILD_HEAVY_SLOT_WAIT_BOUND` line.
**Narrowed promise:** a stage chain whose lane-local `stage-one.ps1` does not pass the deadline (or export the env var) still waits 60 min; the fix lives in the tracked assembler, guard and nightly driver, and a run-dir copy must forward `-HeavySlotDeadlineUtc <chain deadline>`. A very long wait holds the chain open until its deadline instead of failing fast.
**Falsifier:** `pytest tests/coordination/test_build_guard.py -k "staging_deadline or without_a_deadline or from_the_environment or deadline_that_has_passed or deadline_that_is_not"` passes; on `0a553b4e` the first fails with `wait=60m`.
